Where to Stay in Lesotho

Mmelesi Lodge: Mmelesi Lodge in Maseru offers comfortable rooms with free WiFi, air-conditioning, and private bathrooms.Each room includes a tea and coffee maker, free toiletries, and a shower. The lodge features a family-friendly restaurant with a traditional ambiance. Guests can enjoy African, local, barbecue grill, and South African cuisines. Breakfast options include continental, buffet, à la carte, and full English/Irish.The lodge offers an outdoor seating area, garden, and free on-site private parking. Additional amenities include a bar, paid shuttle service, and luggage storage. Mmelesi Lodge is 11 mi from Moshoeshoe International Airport.Couples in particular like the location,they rated it 9.0 for a two-person trip.

Khutsong Lodge: Khutsong Lodge rests at the foothills of the historic Thaba-Bosiu Mountain, home of the founder of the Basotho nation Paramount Chief Moshoeshoe 1 and the final resting place of Lesotho’s Monarchs including King Moshoeshoe 2. The Lodge’s luxury coupled with the destination’s nature-based experiences makes Khutsong Lodge a preferred option for guests seeking culture and wilderness.

Avani Lesotho Hotel & Casino: Avani Lesotho Hotel & Casino offers guests panoramic views of the Maseru city center. It also features a spa, outdoor swimming pool and casino, as well as several restaurants and bars. Set in a modern, sandstone building, the rooms and suites all come with air conditioning, a seating area and satellite TV. They are also equipped with tea-and- coffee making facilities and a safety deposit box. The ensuite bathrooms include a hairdryer and free toiletries. Guests can enjoy buffet breakfasts, lunches and dinners at Nala Café, or enjoy a lite meal and a selection of cakes and coffee at The Pantry. Offering a fitness center and sauna.
