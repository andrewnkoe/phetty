Welcome to Lesotho

Lesotho is known as the “Mountain Kingdom” or “Kingdom in the Sky” due to its high-altitude terrain, dramatic mountain ranges, and pristine natural environment. Its tourism appeal lies in rugged landscapes, rich flora and fauna, and deep cultural traditions.

Lesotho’s tourism centers on its mountain landscapes, cultural heritage, and natural attractions such as Maletsunyane Falls, Katse Dam, and the Maluti Mountains, supported by government initiatives to promote sustainable tourism.

Why Visit Lesotho?

Natural Beauty

The country boasts stunning mountain ranges, including the Maluti Mountains, and offers breathtaking views, waterfalls, and pristine landscapes.

Adventures Activities

Lesotho is known for its outdoors adventures, including skiing at Afriski Mountain Resort, hiking, and 4by4 tours through the challenging Sani Pass.

Cultural Experiences

Visitors can immerse themselves in the vibrant Basotho culture, with opportunities to learn about local traditions, customs and ferstivals.
